Listening Space

A quiet place to talk. An AI listener, here to listen — not to judge, fix, or advise.

Before you start: this is an AI, not a person, and not a crisis service. It listens the way lifeline support workers are trained to — client-centred, no judgment — but it is not affiliated with TELL or any helpline, and nothing here replaces real human support. Nothing you write is stored.
If you're in crisis: in Japan, TELL Lifeline 0800-300-8355 (9am–11pm, English) or chat at telljp.com · emergency 119 / 110. Elsewhere, findahelpline.com.
Conversations are not saved and end when you close the page. This tool can make mistakes; for anything serious, please talk to a person.
